## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for a cutting-edge Senegalese football scouting platform. It merges the electric intensity of African football talent detection with the precision of elite live-match telemetry and FIFA broadcast interfaces. 

### Brand Personality & Emotional Core
- **Electric Athleticism**: Channels the visceral energy of stadium floodlights piercing through pitch-black Dakar nights.
- **Elite Tech-Scouting**: Evokes the rigor of data-driven scouting networks, professional video analytics, and next-gen sports academies.
- **Teranga Pride & Optimism**: Combines warm cultural sovereignty with sharp modern digital athleticism, inspiring young players, scouts, and managers alike.

### Aesthetic Movement: High-Contrast Cyber-Athletic Glassmorphism
The aesthetic relies on extreme luminosity contrast: an ultra-deep, pitch-green canvas layered with frosted glass interfaces, ultra-fine white hairlines, and laser-precise accents of electric lime. The interaction landscape uses dynamic floating elements, immersive phone mockups showcasing match intelligence, and capsule-shaped navigation surfaces that appear suspended above stadium backdrops.

## Colors

The palette is tuned for high visual hierarchy in low-light environments, prioritizing readability during tactical scouting sessions and video reviews.

### Core Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#0d1410`)**: The foundational deep black-green base that anchors the visual plane, eliminating OLED glare while retaining stadium pitch ambiance.
- **Card Surfaces (`#141d17` & translucent variants)**: Structural containers that sit elevated above the base canvas.
- **Primary Accent (`#a8e600`)**: Electric lime/lemon reserved for critical conversion targets, active states, metrics highlights, and visual focal points. Text resting on this background must always use the canvas base `#0d1410` to maintain WCAG AAA compliance.
- **Secondary (`#ffffff`)**: Secondary actions, play heads, and pure-white high-tier highlights.
- **Neutral Foregrounds (`#f2f4ef` & `#9aa89e`)**: Off-white primary text prevents eye fatigue, while muted green-gray provides legible secondary metadata.

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances athletic technological impact with high-density data legibility:

- **Space Grotesk**: Applied to all brand display titles, hero statements, stat values, and interactive pill button labels. Its angular, progressive structural geometry reflects football speed, scouting analytics, and athletic confidence.
- **Inter**: Applied to sustained body text, scout evaluations, tactical summaries, and sub-labels. Its neutral grotesque qualities ensure effortless reading against deep obsidian backgrounds.

All uppercase display headlines should maintain tight negative letter tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and reduced line height for maximum broadcast-style impact.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout architecture employs a centered 12-column fluid grid system bounded by a max container width of 1240px (`77.5rem`).

### Rhythm & Responsive Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with 16px margins. Navigation docks to bottom or compresses into a compact frosted pill bar. Overlapping floating cards stack vertically underneath the primary device mockup.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout with 24px margins. Device stage scales down proportionally with floating stat badges hugging the periphery.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12-column layout with 32px margins and 24px gutters. Hero sections place the phone showcase dead-center with absolute-positioned floating telemetry cards anchoring the lateral axes (`Live Match Updates` to the left, `AI Scout Voice` to the right).

### Structural Containers
- Sections leverage generous vertical breathing room (`space-3xl` / 72px) to let ambient glow cones spread without visual clutter.
- In-component micro spacing utilizes an unbending 4px/8px modular scale for metric alignment, pill buttons, and chip rows.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is built via layered glassmorphic tiers, atmospheric green lighting, and deep structural drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Glow
- **Hero Stadium Spotlight**: An ambient overhead radial gradient (`radial-gradient(closest-side, rgba(168, 230, 0, 0.16), transparent 70%)`) positioned off-screen top-center, establishing a subtle dome of light.
- **Device Video Flare**: A localized secondary radial glow (`rgba(168, 230, 0, 0.12)`) cast from within media players.

### Surface Tiers
1. **Level 0 (Canvas)**: Solid `#0d1410`.
2. **Level 1 (Structural Cards & Drawers)**: Solid `#141d17` or translucent `rgba(20, 29, 23, 0.70)` with a 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
3. **Level 2 (Floating Glass Telemetry)**: Frosted backdrop surface `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` combined with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, a hairline border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, and micro ambient drop shadow (`0 8px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.40)`).
4. **Level 3 (Interactive Floating Dock & Device)**: The phone mockup casts an ultra-deep stage shadow (`0 40px 90px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.55)`), projecting it into the foreground as the interactive focal point.

## Shapes

The design system is defined by an ultra-rounded pill aesthetic for micro/macro interactive elements, contrasted against structural rounded rectangles for display surfaces.

### Radius Token Mapping
- **Total Capsule / Pill (`9999px`)**: All buttons (`.btn-primary`, `.btn-secondary`), main floating navigation container, search capsules, status chips, player trait tags, and video scrubber tracks.
- **Circular (`50%`)**: Media play buttons, scout avatar cutouts, and circular metric icons.
- **Hardware Curves (`32px - 44px`)**: Smartphone bezel borders and inner device canvas wrappers.
- **Card Containers (`16px - 20px`)**: Telemetry overlays, scout evaluation cards, and data popovers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill (`.btn-primary`)**: 
  - Fill: `#a8e600` (Electric Lime).
  - Text: `#0d1410` (Canvas Base), 15px Space Grotesk SemiBold.
  - Border: None.
  - Shape: Capsule (`9999px`), padding `13px 26px`.
  - States: Hover triggers a luminous box shadow `0 0 24px rgba(168, 230, 0, 0.45)`.
- **Secondary Glass Pill (`.btn-secondary`)**:
  - Fill: `#ffffff` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
  - Text: `#f2f4ef` (or `#0d1410` when white-filled).
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
  - Shape: Capsule (`9999px`), padding `13px 26px`.

### Floating Navigation Bar
- **Shell**: Centered floating capsule (`padding: 8px 12px`), background `rgba(13, 20, 16, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Items**: Rounded pill tabs (`padding: 8px 16px`). Active item features full `#a8e600` fill with `#0d1410` text; inactive items utilize `#9aa89e` text with transparent backgrounds shifting to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` on hover.

### Floating Scout & Telemetry Cards
- **Construction**: Translucent glass pane `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`, 16px rounded corners, and hairline outline `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Elements**: 34px circular icon container (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`), title in Space Grotesk 13px bold `#f2f4ef`, and subtitle in Inter 12px `#9aa89e`.

### Telemetry Progress Bars & Match Trackers
- **Trough**: 4px height, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, radius `9999px`.
- **Fill**: `#a8e600` solid with an optional edge glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 10px rgba(168, 230, 0, 0.60)`).

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Surface**: Capsule or 12px radius, fill `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Text**: Space Grotesk 14px `#f2f4ef`, placeholder `#9aa89e`.
- **Focus**: Border shifts to `rgba(168, 230, 0, 0.60)` with soft outer lime glow.

### Scouting Chips & Badges
- **Pill Badges**: Pill-shaped micro-labels with `padding: 6px 12px`, surface `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`, text 11px Inter `#9aa89e`.
- **Accent Announcement Pill**: Border `1px solid rgba(168, 230, 0, 0.40)`, text `#a8e600` with high-luminosity leading icon.
